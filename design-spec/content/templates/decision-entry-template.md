# Decision Entry Template

## Purpose
Standard format for recording decisions in the decision log.

## When to Use
Whenever a decision affects future behavior, files, permissions, or
defaults — typically during end-of-day and reviews.

## Template
```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD — [Decision Title]

**Decision:**
**Context:**
**Reasoning:**
**Approved By:**
**Impacted Files:**
**Follow-Up Needed:**
```

## Completion Checklist
- Dated, newest entries appended under `## Entries`.
- Approved By names who decided (you, or you via `Proceed`).

## Notes
The decision log is append-only; corrections are new entries, not edits.
